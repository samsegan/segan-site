    About

    Bass music for dark rooms.

    Segan is a producer from New York making bass music for warehouses, basements and the walk home after.

    Originals come out under his own name. The edits and bootlegs go up on Segan's Selects, his side account, free to download.

    No rules on tempo. If you need a genre: mostly drum & bass.
